# Prompt Immagini — WhatsApp Business per Hotel e Tour Operator: Il Canale che Converte il 45%

Prompt pronti per **Google Gemini** (Nano Banana / Imagen). Copia il blocco
di testo e incollalo in Gemini senza modifiche.

**Convenzioni brand applicate:** fotografia editoriale, luce naturale,
palette navy `#0B3D61` + teal `#14A3A3`, ambientazione mediterranea,
nessun testo dentro l'immagine.

---

## 1. Featured image (copertina)

- **Formato:** 16:9 — esporta a 1200×675 px
- **Dove va:** Immagine in evidenza di WordPress
- **Nome file consigliato:** `whatsapp-business-hotel-tour-operator-copertina.webp`

**Prompt:**

```
Photorealistic editorial photography. Close three-quarter view of a hotel receptionist standing behind a pale limestone front desk in a sunlit boutique property in Naples, holding a smartphone in both hands mid-reply, a slim monitor and a small espresso cup out of focus beside her. Behind the desk, an open arched doorway reveals a soft-focus Mediterranean courtyard with bougainvillea and warm terracotta tones. Late morning light enters from the left, natural shadows, shallow depth of field, phone screen glowing but with no readable content. Responsive, competent, premium mood. Palette of deep navy blue (#0B3D61), teal accent (#14A3A3) and warm neutral stone tones. 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no logos in the image.
```

**Varianti se il risultato non convince:**
- *Più caldo/umano:* aggiungi in coda `Golden hour lighting, warmer skin tones, softer shadows.`
- *Più corporate:* aggiungi in coda `Cooler color grading, minimalist composition, more negative space.`

**Alt text SEO:** `WhatsApp Business hotel: receptionist che risponde a un ospite dal telefono aziendale`
