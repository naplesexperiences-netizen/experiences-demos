# Prompt Immagini — Channel Manager per Tour Operator: Gestisci 100+ Tour da 1 Dashboard

Prompt pronti per **Google Gemini** (Nano Banana / Imagen). Copia il blocco
di testo e incollalo in Gemini senza modifiche.

**Convenzioni brand applicate:** fotografia editoriale, luce naturale,
palette navy `#0B3D61` + teal `#14A3A3`, ambientazione mediterranea,
nessun testo dentro l'immagine.

---

## 1. Featured image (copertina)

- **Formato:** 16:9 — esporta a 1200×675 px
- **Dove va:** Immagine in evidenza di WordPress
- **Nome file consigliato:** `channel-manager-tour-operator-copertina.webp`

**Prompt:**

```
Photorealistic editorial photography. Three-quarter view from behind a tour operator standing at a wide pale limestone desk in a bright office above the Gulf of Naples, one hand resting on the bezel of a large monitor whose screen glows but shows no readable content. On the desk a ceramic mug, a printed map of the Campania coast and a neat stack of folders. Through the tall arched window, Vesuvius and the sea in soft morning haze above terracotta rooftops. Natural side light, long soft shadows, shallow depth of field, composed and confident mood. Palette deep navy blue (#0B3D61), teal accent (#14A3A3), warm neutral tones. 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no logos in the image.
```

**Varianti se il risultato non convince:**
- *Più caldo/umano:* aggiungi in coda `Golden hour lighting, warmer skin tones, softer shadows.`
- *Più corporate:* aggiungi in coda `Cooler color grading, minimalist composition, more negative space.`

**Alt text SEO:** `Channel manager tour operator: dashboard unica in un ufficio con vista sul golfo di Napoli`
