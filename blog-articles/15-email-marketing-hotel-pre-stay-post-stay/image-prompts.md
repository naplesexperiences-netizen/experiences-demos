# Prompt Immagini — Email Marketing per Hotel: Pre-Stay, Post-Stay e Recupero delle Prenotazioni Abbandonate

Prompt pronti per **Google Gemini** (Nano Banana / Imagen). Copia il blocco
di testo e incollalo in Gemini senza modifiche.

**Convenzioni brand applicate:** fotografia editoriale, luce naturale,
palette navy `#0B3D61` + teal `#14A3A3`, ambientazione mediterranea,
nessun testo dentro l'immagine.

---

## 1. Featured image (copertina)

- **Formato:** 16:9 — esporta a 1200×675 px
- **Dove va:** Immagine in evidenza di WordPress
- **Nome file consigliato:** `email-marketing-hotel-pre-stay-post-stay-copertina.webp`

**Prompt:**

```
Photorealistic editorial photography. Over-the-shoulder three-quarter view of a hotel revenue manager seated at a pale limestone desk inside a sunlit boutique hotel office in Sorrento, hands resting on a slim open laptop, a smartphone and a small espresso cup beside it. Through the open arched window behind, a soft-focus Mediterranean seascape with bougainvillea spilling over a terrace railing. Early morning golden light rakes across the desk, long gentle shadows, shallow depth of field. Calm, competent, premium mood. Palette of deep navy blue (#0B3D61), teal accent (#14A3A3) and warm neutral stone tones. 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no logos in the image.
```

**Varianti se il risultato non convince:**
- *Più caldo/umano:* aggiungi in coda `Golden hour lighting, warmer skin tones, softer shadows.`
- *Più corporate:* aggiungi in coda `Cooler color grading, minimalist composition, more negative space.`

**Alt text SEO:** `Email marketing hotel: revenue manager al lavoro in un ufficio con vista mare`
