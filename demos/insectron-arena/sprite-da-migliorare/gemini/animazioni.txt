TESTA COMUNE — va in cima a tutti e quattro
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The attached image is the reference for this character. Keep it EXACTLY:
same body plan, same proportions, same {COLORE} body colour, same dark
outline weight, same flat shading with one soft highlight, same cartoon
mascot style, three-quarter side view facing right.

This is {NOME}, of the {FAMIGLIA} family: {FIRMA}.

Draw ONE strip of {N} animation frames, left to right, evenly spaced, each
frame the same square size, on a flat #2a3147 background. No text, no
numbers, no frame borders, no captions anywhere in the image.

THE CHARACTER MUST NOT CHANGE BETWEEN FRAMES. Same size, same colours,
same markings, same number of limbs. Only the pose and the effects change.
Keep the feet on the same ground line in every frame, and keep the
character at the same distance from the camera: these frames are played
one after the other, and anything that drifts reads as a glitch.


1. ATTACCO — 6 fotogrammi
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. ready stance, weight on the back legs, {ARMA} ready
  2. wind-up: the whole body leans back, {ARMA} drawn back
  3. lunge forward, {ARMA} leading, with motion streaks behind it
  4. impact: {ARMA} driven into an unseen target at the right edge, a
     bright yellow-white burst at the point of contact
  5. follow-through: body low and forward past the strike, a small dust
     puff at the feet, a white arc tracing the path of {ARMA}
  6. back to the ready stance of frame 1

{ARMA} e' quello con cui quella famiglia colpisce: la lama dei Lamidi, il
corno dei Bastidi, la canna dei Bombardidi, la pala dei Voltidi, le falci
dei Falcidi, le tenaglie dei Tenaglidi, la testa a trivella dei Trivellidi,
le ali di Silfidi e Ventalidi, il guscio dei Coccinidi, la palla dei
Sferidi, le zampe posteriori dei Corsidi, la scarica dei Tonantidi.
Senza, il prompt direbbe "weapon" anche a chi un'arma non ce l'ha.

The force comes from the anticipation in frame 2 and the recovery in
frame 5. Frame 4 is the only one with the burst.


2. COLPITO — 4 fotogrammi
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. recoil: head back, body compressed, pained expression
  2. the whole character washed out to near-white, as if lit from inside
     by the impact — keep the silhouette identical to frame 1
  3. staggering, off balance, small coloured stars circling the head
  4. upright again, shaken, eyes narrowed

Frame 2 is a flash: same shape, different colour, nothing else.


3. VITTORIA — 6 fotogrammi
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. both forelimbs raised, chest out, delighted
  2. a small jump, feet off the ground, limbs up
  3. at the top of the jump, surrounded by small coloured confetti
  4. a courteous bow, one limb folded across the body
  5. upright again with warm sparkles around the head
  6. a final raised fist, pleased with itself

Happy, not manic. This is a creature that has just won a duel, not a
cartoon on a sugar rush.


4. SCONFITTA — 6 fotogrammi
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. hunched, exhausted, limbs hanging
  2. a dizzy spiral over one eye, swaying
  3. knees buckling, body sagging forward
  4. flat on the ground, side view, limbs splayed
  5. on the ground with coloured stars circling above the head
  6. on the ground, eyes closed, a slow spiral of stars settling

Defeated and tired, never gruesome: no blood, no injury, no distress.
The character keeps its dignity.


NOTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Lo sfondo si chiede PIENO (#2a3147), non trasparente. L'alfa si toglie
dopo, con uno scontorno deterministico: chiedere la trasparenza al modello
e' una variabile in piu' che non serve, perche' lo sfondo e' un colore
piatto e noto e si rimuove senza ambiguita'.
